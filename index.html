Northern STARS, summer camp

Robot Camp Games

Five games that kids played with a Reachy Mini robot at the Northern STARS summer camp. The robot watches, listens and reacts: it reads ArUco markers and body movement, runs games with buttons on a Raspberry Pi 5, and talks through a local LLM.
